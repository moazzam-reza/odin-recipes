Dubious Animal Meat

Description

Mysterious, dubious animal meat.

Ingredients

  • Dubious animal meat
  • Courage
  • Resistant stomach
  • Powerful bowels
  1. Muster up the courage to look at the dubious animal meat
  2. Muster up the courage to pick it up
  3. Muster up the courage to put it on a stick and roast it over the fire. Ignore the worms slithering out of it like rats on a sinking ship.
  4. Stare at it long and hard once it's charred by the flame, the globules of rotten fat dripping down the stick and eroding it. Will you do this to survive?
  5. Steel your will. Be resolved. Eat it.
  6. Pray you survive
  7. Endure the suffering imposed upon your bowels
  8. Survive.
  9. Develop immunity to dubious animal meat
  10. Proceed to be the only human able to eat dubious animal meat
  11. No more food competition. You are the top caveman, and may glut yourself as you please. Enjoy the attention of the cave ladies as they marvel at your large, dubious-meat filled belly.
