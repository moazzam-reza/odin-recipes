Cow Recipe

Description

This is my delicious and absolutely scrum diddly garlic-olive-oil-rosemary basted cow recipe. Most steak recipes use butter with no regard for us lactose intolerants. I'm here to save you all with olive oil.

Ingredients

  • Beef -- steak cut
  • Olive oil
  • Rosemary
  • Garlic
  • Salt
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  1. Coat steak with olive oil to serve as a binder
  2. Season generously with salt and pepper
  3. Get a stainless steel or cast iron pan ripping hot
  4. Sear that steak up until you get a nice crust
  5. Lower the heat and pour in some olive oil. Place garlic and rosemary in olive oil
  6. Baste the steak with the garlic and rosemary infused olive oil with a spoon until it reaches internal temp of your liking
  7. Take out of pan and let rest for 5-10 minutes
  8. Enjoy!
