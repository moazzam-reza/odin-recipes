Boiled Donkey

Description

Nothing better then some good ol' boiled donkey.

Ingredients

  • Donkey Meat
  • Filthy creek water
  1. Fill a pot with hot, filthy, muddy creek water and boil it
  2. Insert cubes of donkey meat
  3. Boil for 2 hours
  4. Enjoy unseasoned, to get the real donkey experience.
